An autonomous robot that maintains and monitors roadside green strips, so they can serve as connected habitats.

- Insect-friendly mowing and trimming around obstacles.
- Sowing of native plants.
- Image recognition to spot invasive species.

Funded by the BMFTR (Digital GreenTech, 02/2026 – 01/2028). Partners: Paltech GmbH, Kempten University of Applied Sciences and the L3S Research Center, Leibniz University Hannover.
