The breast cancer use case of the Leibniz AI Lab, a BMBF-funded international AI research lab (2020 – 2023) at the L3S Research Center, Leibniz University Hannover, in cooperation with the Department of Gynaecology at Hannover Medical School (MHH).

- Machine learning algorithms that predict whether a patient will respond to neoadjuvant treatment.
- Goal: help decide which patients gain more from neoadjuvant therapy and which from surgery.

Sources: [L3S project archive](https://www.l3s.de/research-at-l3s/all-projects/project-archive/leibnizkilabor/), [Leibniz AI Lab research page (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20260119175736/https://leibniz-ai-lab.de/research/).
